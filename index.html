Demo and test status. Flux is currently a demo/test application. Do not submit real identity documents, banking details, or other sensitive personal information to a test build. Some features require external services to be configured before they operate.

1. Scope

This policy describes the Flux passenger and rider mobile apps and their supporting backend. It is based on the application’s publicly available source as of 9 October 2026. It explains categories the app is designed to handle when features are used; it does not claim every optional feature is active in every demo build.

2. Information Flux handles

Account and sign-inMobile phone number, one-time sign-in code or authentication token, name, profile photo, role, and optional saved home or work address. Phone verification uses Firebase Authentication when configured.
Rider onboardingThe rider data model includes date of birth, gender, home address, vehicle details, driving licence and other identity/vehicle document URLs, bank account number, IFSC code, and account-holder name. Uploaded photos or documents may be sent to configured Amazon S3 storage.
Rides and communicationPickup and drop addresses and coordinates, ride status and history, bids and fare information, route information, ratings or reviews, recipient name and phone for parcel flows, in-app chat, and complaints when those features are used.
LocationThe mobile apps request device location for maps and ride workflows. The backend model can store a rider’s current latitude, longitude, and last location update. Pickup and drop coordinates are stored with bookings.
Notifications and logsA device push token can be stored for Firebase Cloud Messaging. Notification content and status can be stored. Application authentication logs in the current backend code can include phone numbers and errors.

The current payment service creates free rider subscriptions. Its Stripe payment path is disabled, so this policy does not describe active card processing by Flux.

3. Why this information is used

  • Register and verify passenger or rider accounts.
  • Offer and coordinate ride, bidding, pickup, tracking, chat, and notification features.
  • Review rider documents and vehicle details where onboarding is enabled.
  • Respond to support or complaint messages and troubleshoot the demo.

4. External services and sharing

Configured features can send relevant information to Google Firebase for phone authentication and push messaging; Google Maps services for maps, places, or directions; and Amazon S3 for uploaded files. Information may also pass through the hosting, database, and infrastructure services used to operate a test build. The exact deployment and vendors may differ between builds.

Flux may show ride and contact details to the passenger or rider involved in a booking where needed for the workflow. The public source does not establish any sale of personal information; this policy makes no claim about practices beyond the demo described here.

5. Security

The source includes phone verification, authentication tokens, and access-control checks. No internet service is risk-free, and the public source does not establish a specific encryption, audit, or certification standard for every deployment. Avoid entering real identity and bank data into test builds.

6. Retention and deletion

Flux has no fixed data retention period and no formally defined or automated process for deleting a test account and its associated data. Manual removal from the backend, Firebase, or S3 is not yet established as a standard procedure. Test data may remain in configured systems until it is removed. A documented workflow for verifying requests, removing data, and defining retention timelines is planned. You can email the private contact channel below to raise a request or ask about your data, but a specific deletion timeline cannot currently be promised. Do not post your phone number or documents in a public GitHub issue.

7. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to grant location or notification permission on your device, though related features may not work. For questions about access, correction, or deletion of test data, email the contact below. A standardized process for verifying and fulfilling these requests has not yet been implemented. Please do not email identity-document images unless specifically requested.

8. Children

Flux is intended as a ride-booking demo, not a service directed to children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to a test build, contact us privately.

9. Policy updates

Changes to Flux’s data handling or this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Review the page when using a new build.
